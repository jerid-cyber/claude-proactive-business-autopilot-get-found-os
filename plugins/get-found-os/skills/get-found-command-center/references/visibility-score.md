# Visibility Score (0-100)

The single number the agent works to raise. Score each pillar from evidence, never from guesses; if a pillar
cannot be measured yet, mark it "unmeasured" and score it 0 until it is.

| Pillar | Points | Full marks look like | Main skills |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maps & listings | 15 | Top 3 map pack on core terms; GBP complete and posting weekly; NAP 95%+ consistent; Apple/Bing verified | 2, 5, 9, 12, 63 |
| AI answers | 20 | Named in 40%+ of buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI; 95%+ accurate | 4, 11, 17, 49, 26 |
| Search | 15 | 90%+ of buyer intents mapped to a page; priority pages indexed; target terms top 10 | 7, 10, 15, 23, 46 |
| Reviews & proof | 15 | 4.5+ on every site that matters; a new review every 2 weeks per site; 100% replies in 24h | 3, 37, 43, 52 |
| Social & video | 10 | Active, consistent profiles; search-optimized video; profile-to-site clicks tracked | 24, 25, 32, 33 |
| Paid | 10 | Tracking verified; cost per lead at or below target; no wasted spend on non-converting terms | 20, 60, 64, 85 |
| Conversion | 15 | Lead response under 5 minutes; clear offer; site conversion at or above industry median | 13, 14, 18, 36, 66 |

Score rules:
- Record every pillar score with the evidence and date in `get-found/scoreboard.md`.
- Re-score quarterly (full) and monthly (AI answers and Maps pillars).
- Report the change against the first recorded score, in points and in plain language.
